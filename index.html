Resume Reformatting Tool
morganisms: resumefit

Reformat a resume

Add a Word template and a resume. ResumeFit reads the resume's content, lets you check how it was split into sections, and writes it into the template's fonts, spacing and layout as a new Word file.

Template

A Word file (.docx or .dotx) in the look you want. A finished resume in that style works, or a template with {{placeholders}}.

Resume

The resume to reformat. Text-based PDFs and Word files work; scanned PDFs have no text to read.

Paste text instead

Check the content

Fix anything that was read into the wrong place. Changes show in the preview.

In each section: ## starts a role or school line (dates at the end are right-aligned), ### a second line such as company and location, - a bullet. Other lines are paragraphs.

Default style

An approximation of fonts and spacing. Sidebars, page layout, headers and footers come from the template in the downloaded file.

Files stay in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
